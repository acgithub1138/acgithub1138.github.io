Privacy Policy for Bowshot: Stick Archery

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Bowshot: Stick Archery is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses. The short version: we have no accounts, no servers and no way to see who you are. The only data collected is collected by Google's advertising service to show ads.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run any servers, and we never receive your name, email, contacts, photos, location or game data.

Your progress and iCloud

Your level, the look of your archer and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also stores them in your own iCloud account through Apple so they follow you to your other devices. That data stays in your iCloud account, and we cannot read it. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a full-screen ad after every second finished game. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to fight fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • a device identifier for apps (not your name or phone number)
  • approximate location, worked out from your internet address
  • information about ads shown and tapped, and how the app is used
  • crash and performance information

We ask Google for non-personalized ads, and the game does not ask permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Ads are chosen from things like the game itself and your approximate location, not from a profile of you. In the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent message may ask for your choices before ads are shown.

Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. After you buy it, the game shows no more ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
